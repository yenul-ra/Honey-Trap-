What am I seeing?

Every bee has limited, private knowledge. Bees only exchange information with bees inside their communication radius. When they meet, they swap which cells have been explored, which are dead ends, and (if known) the exit route. Nothing is broadcast globally and no shortest path is computed for the swarm.

Red line = communication; thicker = more contacts. Orange = new-area info, green = exit info. Amber floor = explored by at least one bee. Blue floor (Selected bee) = what that bee knows. The 3D mind map shows who talks to whom: click a node to focus a bee, click a line to read every communication between that pair.
